    Welcome to Chess Amateur

    Playing modes

    Play in three modes: Casual for relaxed games with no rating on the line, Rated for games that move your Chess Amateur rating, and FIDE for competitive play seeded from your FIDE rating.

    FIDE mode is for serious play

    FIDE mode is meant for serious play because FIDE-rated games generate statistical data about the player. Choose it when you want your results to count toward your profile's statistics.

    My Games & collections

    Every account game is saved under My Games, where you can replay, filter, and organize games into nestable collections.

    Custom positions

    In Casual mode you can set up any position with the board editor and start a game from there.

    Train

    The Train tab serves rated tactics puzzles tuned to your puzzle rating so you improve at the right level.

    Dashboard

    The Dashboard charts your gameplay and puzzle ratings over time so you can track your progress. That's it, enjoy!
